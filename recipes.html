10 Easy Indian Recipes for Beginners

Cooking Indian food does not have to be difficult. These 10 easy recipes are perfect for beginners and use simple ingredients with easy-to-follow procedures.

Breakfast

Recipe 1 – Vegetable Upma

A savory and comforting semolina breakfast with mixed vegetables and warm spices.

Cooking Time: 20 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rava/semolina
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon urad dal
  • Few curry leaves
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste

Procedure

Heat oil in a pan. Add the tempering ingredients, sauté vegetables, add water and rava, and cook until soft.

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add urad dal and curry leaves.
  4. Add chopped onion, carrot, and green chilli.
  5. Sauté until the vegetables become slightly soft.
  6. Add water and salt.
  7. Bring the water to a boil.
  8. Slowly add rava while continuously stirring.
  9. Cook until the water is absorbed and the upma becomes soft.
  10. Serve hot.

Lunch

Recipe 2 – Lemon Rice

Zesty lemon rice with peanuts, tempered spices, and a refreshing citrus finish.

Cooking Time: 15 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon urad dal
  • 1 green chilli
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon peanuts
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste

Procedure

Tempering seeds, lentils, curry leaves, and peanuts add flavor before mixing with rice and lemon juice.

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add mustard seeds.
  3. Add urad dal, peanuts, green chilli, and curry leaves.
  4. Add turmeric powder.
  5. Add cooked rice and salt.
  6. Mix everything gently.
  7. Turn off the flame.
  8. Add fresh lemon juice.
  9. Mix well and serve.

Breakfast

Recipe 3 – Vegetable Poha

A light, quick breakfast of flattened rice cooked with colorful vegetables and spices.

Cooking Time: 15 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups poha
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 tablespoon peanuts
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste

Procedure

Wash the poha, temper it with mustard, curry leaves, and vegetables, then gently toss until soft and ready.

  1. Wash poha gently and drain the water.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add mustard seeds and peanuts.
  4. Add curry leaves and green chilli.
  5. Add chopped onion and vegetables.
  6. Sauté until soft.
  7. Add turmeric and salt.
  8. Add washed poha.
  9. Mix gently.
  10. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.

Lunch

Recipe 4 – Dal Tadka

Classic yellow lentil curry finished with a fragrant garlic and cumin tempering.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Few curry leaves or coriander leaves
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water

Procedure

Cook the dal until soft, then prepare a simple tempering and pour it over the lentils for rich flavor.

  1. Wash the dal thoroughly.
  2. Add dal, turmeric, tomato, and water to a pressure cooker.
  3. Cook until the dal becomes soft.
  4. Mash the cooked dal.
  5. Heat oil or ghee in a pan.
  6. Add cumin seeds and garlic.
  7. Add onion and green chilli.
  8. Sauté until the onion becomes golden.
  9. Add the cooked dal and salt.
  10. Boil for a few minutes.
  11. Garnish with coriander leaves.

Breakfast

Recipe 5 – Masala Dosa

Crispy dosa paired with spiced potato filling and served with chutney or sambar.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • Dosa batter
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
  • Oil
  • Salt

Procedure

Prepare the potato masala, cook thin dosa, and fill it with savory potato mixture before folding.

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
  4. Add onion and green chilli.
  5. Add turmeric and salt.
  6. Add mashed potatoes and mix well.
  7. Heat a dosa pan.
  8. Pour dosa batter and spread it thinly.
  9. Add a little oil.
  10. Place potato masala in the center.
  11. Fold the dosa and serve hot with chutney or sambar.

Dinner

Recipe 6 – Vegetable Biryani

Fragrant rice cooked with vegetables, herbs, and biryani spices for a rich one-pot meal.

Cooking Time: 40 minutes Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 potato
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Coriander and mint leaves

Procedure

Cook a flavorful vegetable base, add soaked rice and water, and finish with herbs for aromatic biryani.

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add sliced onion and sauté.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste.
  5. Add tomato and vegetables.
  6. Add biryani masala and salt.
  7. Add soaked rice.
  8. Add the required amount of water.
  9. Cover and cook until the rice becomes soft.
  10. Garnish with coriander and mint leaves.

Lunch

Recipe 7 – Tomato Rice

Simple and flavorful rice dish cooked with chopped tomatoes and warming spices.

Cooking Time: 20 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt

Procedure

Sauté onion and tomatoes with spices, then mix in cooked rice and heat through.

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add curry leaves and green chilli.
  3. Add chopped onion.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes.
  6. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  7. Add turmeric, chilli powder, and salt.
  8. Add cooked rice.
  9. Mix everything well.
  10. Cook for another 2–3 minutes and serve.

Recipe 8 – Aloo Paratha

Stuffed Indian flatbread filled with spiced potato and cooked until golden brown.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green chilli
  • Coriander leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Oil or ghee

Procedure

Prepare the dough, make a potato filling with spices, stuff it inside the paratha, and cook on both sides.

  1. Prepare dough using wheat flour and water.
  2. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  3. Add onion, green chilli, coriander, spices, and salt.
  4. Mix the potato filling.
  5. Take a small portion of dough.
  6. Roll it slightly.
  7. Add potato filling and close the dough.
  8. Roll it into a flat paratha.
  9. Heat a pan.
  10. Cook the paratha on both sides with oil or ghee.
  11. Serve hot with curd or pickle.

Dinner

Recipe 9 – Vegetable Fried Rice

Quick stir-fried rice with vegetables, soy sauce, and a peppery finish.

Cooking Time: 20 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 cup cabbage
  • 1/2 cup beans
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon pepper powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt

Procedure

Stir-fry vegetables and toss with rice, soy sauce, and pepper for a quick, savory meal.

  1. Heat oil in a large pan.
  2. Add chopped onion.
  3. Add carrot, cabbage, and beans.
  4. Stir-fry the vegetables on high heat.
  5. Add cooked rice.
  6. Add soy sauce and pepper.
  7. Add salt if required.
  8. Mix everything well.
  9. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  10. Serve hot.

Dinner

Recipe 10 – Vegetable Sambar

A comforting South Indian lentil stew with vegetables, tamarind, and warming spices.

Cooking Time: 35 minutes Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 potato
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons sambar powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • Tamarind
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Procedure

Cook the dal and vegetables, add sambar powder and tamarind, then temper with mustard seeds and curry leaves.

  1. Wash and cook the dal until soft.
  2. Chop the vegetables.
  3. Cook the vegetables separately until tender.
  4. Add cooked dal to the vegetables.
  5. Add sambar powder and turmeric.
  6. Add tamarind water.
  7. Add salt and boil well.
  8. Heat oil in another pan.
  9. Add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
  10. Add the tempering to the sambar.
  11. Mix well and serve hot with rice, idli, or dosa.
